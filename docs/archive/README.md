# Historical material and retention

Current policy is defined only by `docs/architecture/`, `docs/operations/`, `docs/decisions/`, `docs/repository/`, and the binding project instructions in `AGENTS.md`.

The earlier local `docs/autonomy/`, `docs/repo/`, `docs/israel-research/`, and `docs/superpowers/` materials were not part of the public baseline. They include a mix of superseded proposals, live provider receipts, operational artifacts, and candidate-specific research. They remain on disk, ignored by Git, and were not deleted or copied into the product repository. This deliberate exclusion preserves the original local evidence without publishing private or unreviewed content.

When an older proposal is later cleared for publication, copy only the non-sensitive, still-useful evidence here with a clear `Historical / superseded` heading and a link to its current decision or architecture page. Never make an archived proposal authoritative by omission of its status.
